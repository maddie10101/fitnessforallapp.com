Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fitness For All ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Fitness For All iPhone app (the "App") and this website (together, the "Service"). Fitness For All is run by Maddie Sanders.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us, such as:

  • Account and contact info: your name or username and email address.
  • Profile and fitness info: things like your age, fitness level, goals, and measurements you choose to enter (for example height and weight for BMI and BMR estimates).
  • Activity you log: workouts, habits, challenges, personal records, meals and other entries.
  • Photos you choose to add: progress photos and meal photos. The App only uses your camera or photo library when you choose to take or upload a picture.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.

Information collected automatically, such as how you use the App (for example which screens and features you use), device and app version information, and identifiers used to keep you signed in and to run the Service.

Purchase information. If you buy a paid plan, we receive information about the purchase (such as the plan and its status). App Store payments are processed by Apple. We don't receive or store your full card number.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service, including your plans, logs, progress tools and estimates
  • To personalize your experience, for example suggesting workouts for your level
  • To process purchases and manage paid plans
  • To run optional features you choose to use, like photo meal logging or body scan
  • To respond to support requests and send important account or service messages
  • To understand how the App is used, fix bugs and improve it
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent misuse, and to meet legal obligations

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your health and fitness information for advertising.

3. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Service providers who help us operate it, such as app hosting and database providers, payment processors, analytics, and AI services that power optional features like photo meal logging and the meal-prep chat. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Apple, which handles App Store downloads and purchases under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or us.
  • If the Service changes hands: information may be transferred as part of that, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

4. Health and fitness information

Information like your workouts, measurements, meals and progress photos is personal. We use it only to provide features you use, and we don't sell it or use it for advertising. Calorie, BMI, BMR and macro figures are estimates from published methods and are not medical advice.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When it's no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can view and update much of your information in the App, or ask us for a copy.
  • Delete: you can ask us to delete your account and personal information by emailing fitnessforall1010@gmail.com.
  • Photos and camera: you can change camera and photo access any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Emails: if we send optional updates, you can unsubscribe at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law. We'll respond to requests as required by applicable law.

7. Children

Children under 13 should use Fitness For All only with a parent or guardian's permission and involvement. We don't knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us information without that consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of storing or sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. This website

This website (fitnessforallapp.com) is a simple information site. It doesn't have accounts, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (like IP address and pages visited) for security and reliability. If you email us, we use your message to reply.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here and change the "Last updated" date. If changes are significant, we'll let you know in the App or by email.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Email fitnessforall1010@gmail.com.
