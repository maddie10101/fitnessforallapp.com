Common questions

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

If you subscribed through the App Store, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and choose Fitness For All. You can also follow Apple's instructions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought a plan but don't see my features

Make sure you're signed in with the same account you used to buy, then close and reopen the app. If it still doesn't work, email us with the email on your account and the date you purchased.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email fitnessforall1010@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that in your Apple settings too.

Is Fitness For All on Android?

Right now Fitness For All is an iPhone app (iOS 18.6 or later).

Health and safety

Fitness For All gives general fitness guidance and isn't medical advice. Stop if anything hurts, and check with a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an injury or health condition. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
