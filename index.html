FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Fitness For All free?

Fitness For All is free to download. Some features, including meal logging, are part of optional Premium plans. Any price is shown in the app before you buy, and nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.

I've never worked out before. Is this for me?

Yes. That's exactly who it was built for. You start by picking your level, and beginner plans come with demo videos and clear sets and reps, so you always know what to do next.

I'm over 60. Are there workouts for me?

Yes. There are senior-friendly, lower-impact sessions you can do at your own pace. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before starting.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. You can choose home workouts that need no equipment, or gym programs if you have access to one.

Which devices does it work on?

Fitness For All is an iPhone app. It needs iOS 18.6 or later.

How do I cancel a paid plan?

If you subscribed through the App Store, you can manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple has step-by-step instructions. Need help? Email fitnessforall1010@gmail.com.

Is this medical advice?

No. Fitness For All gives general fitness guidance. Calorie, BMI and macro numbers are estimates from published methods, and the sources are shown in the app. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Who makes Fitness For All?

Fitness For All was created by Maddie, a 16-year-old founder who wanted a fitness app that doesn't leave beginners out. Feedback and feature ideas are always welcome.
